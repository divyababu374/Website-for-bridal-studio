About Us

About Indhu Bridal Studio & Beauty Care

At Indhu Bridal Studio & Beauty Care, we believe every bride deserves to look elegant, confident, and beautiful on her special day. Based in Alangayam, Tamil Nadu, we specialize in bridal makeup, bridal hairstyling, saree draping, mehendi artistry, and bridal jewellery styling. Our commitment to quality, attention to detail, and personalized service ensures every client enjoys a memorable beauty experience.
